Our craft · The art of shringar

Adorned slowly, the way it was always done.

Shringar is the old Indian habit of adorning oneself with care: a hand mirror, a lit lamp, a chest of jewellery opened for the evening. Every piece here is made for that ritual, at a worktable, with beads, thread and brass-tone findings.

Before you order

Our name is a raga: hamsa, the swan; dhwani, sound. We made these pieces to be worn at the end of a day, by lamplight. A few honest notes about handmade work follow.
