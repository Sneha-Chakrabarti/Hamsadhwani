Our craft · The art of shringar

Adorned slowly, the way it was always done.

Shringar is the old habit of adorning oneself with care. A hand mirror. A brass comb worn smooth. A lamp low enough to be kind. Every piece here is made for that hour, by hand, from beads, thread and brass-tone findings.

Before you order

Hamsadhwani is a raga: hamsa, the swan; dhwani, sound. It belongs to the end of the day, when things grow quiet. A few plain words on handmade work follow.

i.

Each piece differs

Shape, colour and finish vary a little, as brushstrokes do. Yours will not be an exact copy of the sample.

ii.

Delivery may be delayed

Pieces are made to order. Handmade work keeps its own hours.

iii.

Refund if not delivered

If we cannot deliver, the full amount is returned to you.

iv.

No returns after delivery

Once delivered, an order cannot be returned or refunded.
